# Semicolyn Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026

Semicolyn, by True Positive LLC, does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we run no server the app talks to.

## Connections

The app connects only to the servers you choose. Your sessions go directly between your device and those servers; we never see them. If you turn on log streaming in Settings, Diagnostics, logs go to the server you enter, and nowhere else.

## Your data stays with you

Saved hosts, keys, passwords, settings and learned suggestions are stored on your device, encrypted where sensitive. Keys and passwords sync through iCloud Keychain if you use it. The app never learns what you type at password prompts or values that look like secrets.

## Apple

If you share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details; the app checks purchases on your device.

## Deleting your data

Settings, Suggestions & Privacy has "Clear learned suggestions" and "Erase All Data". Since we hold nothing about you, there is nothing for us to delete.
